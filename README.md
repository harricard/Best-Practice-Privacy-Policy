# Privacy Policy — Best Practices Chatbot

**Version 1.0 · Last updated: 2026-05-19**

## 1. Overview

Best Practices Chatbot is an internal, business-to-business knowledge-retrieval tool. It is operated by **Onni Contracting Ltd.** ("Onni", "we", "us", or "our") on behalf of the Onni Group of Companies. The application is delivered as a native iOS app distributed through Apple TestFlight and the Apple App Store.

Best Practices Chatbot is not a consumer service. Accounts are provisioned by an administrator; there is no self-signup. The application is intended exclusively for authorised personnel of the Onni Group of Companies and approved collaborators. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through the application, why we collect it, how we store and share it, and what choices our users have.

**Our role.** Onni Contracting Ltd. is the controller of the personal information collected through Best Practices Chatbot, including authentication and account data (your work email address, your display name, your assigned role and approval status, and related session information) and the queries and usage events generated by your use of the App. The construction best-practice documents made searchable through the App are property of the Onni Group and do not constitute personal information about any user. Requests relating to your account, your access, or this Privacy Policy should be directed to our Privacy Officer (see Section 13).

## 2. Information we collect

All data collected by Best Practices Chatbot is linked to an authenticated user account and is collected solely to operate the knowledge-retrieval workflow. We do not use any of this data for advertising, cross-app tracking, or profiling. The application contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

### 2.1 Identity and account data

- **Email address**, used to authenticate the user (via Microsoft Entra single sign-on or, where enabled, email and password) and to attribute queries to a user account.
- **Display name**, supplied by your identity provider when you sign in, and attached to your account record and to usage-log entries.
- **Role and approval status.** Your assigned permission level within the App (standard user or administrator) and your approval status (pending, approved, or blocked). These determine which features and which documents you can access.

### 2.2 Queries and usage data

When you use the App we record application events that describe how the App is being used:

- the questions or search queries you submit to the assistant;
- documents and figures you open or view in the App;
- which source documents were returned by retrieval for a query, and which were cited in the resulting answer;
- timestamps and request latencies for these events.

These events are stored with your user identifier and the relevant document identifier. They are used to operate the App, to support you, to monitor service health, and to improve retrieval quality. They are not used for advertising or profiling.

### 2.3 Session and operational data

- **Authentication tokens.** On iOS, authentication tokens are stored in the device's secure keychain. Session tokens identify your active session to the backend; they are not shared with third parties.
- **Server logs.** Our hosting and inference providers (Supabase, OpenRouter, Mistral AI, Microsoft) generate operational logs that may include IP addresses, request paths, status codes, and rate-limit events. These logs are used to operate and secure the service.

### 2.4 Information we do not collect

We do not collect device identifiers for advertising (IDFA / Google AID), location data, contacts, health data, financial data, browsing history outside the App, or sensitive personal information.

We do not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs in the App, and we do not engage in cross-app or cross-site tracking of any kind.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share personal information with third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

## 3. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

- authenticate users and enforce role-based and approval-based access control;
- retrieve documents from the corpus, generate answers to your queries, and display source citations;
- administer access, including approving, suspending, or removing accounts;
- maintain a usage log so administrators can support you and improve the quality and coverage of the corpus;
- operate, secure, and improve the service, including rate-limiting abusive traffic, debugging errors, and preventing fraud or unauthorised access;
- comply with applicable legal and regulatory obligations.

## 4. Legal bases (PIPEDA, BC PIPA, GDPR)

Best Practices Chatbot is operated from Canada and processes personal information under the federal Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and the British Columbia Personal Information Protection Act (BC PIPA). Because Best Practices Chatbot is a business-to-business tool used in the context of an employment or contractor relationship, the personal information we handle is largely work-product information and business contact information, for which a more limited consent regime applies.

Where the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the United Kingdom GDPR applies to a user, our legal bases for processing are: (a) performance of a contract with the user's employer, (b) our legitimate interest in operating an internal knowledge-retrieval tool, and (c) compliance with legal obligations. Best Practices Chatbot is not directed to or intended for use by children, and we do not knowingly process personal information of anyone under the age of majority.

## 5. Third-party processors

Best Practices Chatbot relies on the following sub-processors to operate the service. We do not engage any third party that is not listed here, and none of these sub-processors uses your information for their own purposes.

- **Supabase, Inc.** (United States), authentication, application database, server-side access controls (row-level security), and file storage for source documents. [supabase.com/privacy](https://supabase.com/privacy)
- **OpenRouter, Inc.** (United States), large-language-model inference for chat answers, embeddings, and reranking. Your query text and the document passages retrieved for it are forwarded to the underlying model provider via OpenRouter to generate an answer. [openrouter.ai/privacy](https://openrouter.ai/privacy)
- **Mistral AI** (France), optical character recognition during one-time ingestion of source documents into the corpus. Mistral does not process end-user queries. [mistral.ai/terms#privacy-policy](https://mistral.ai/terms#privacy-policy)
- **Microsoft Corporation** (Microsoft Entra ID, multi-region), single sign-on identity provider. Microsoft authenticates the user and returns an email and display name to Best Practices Chatbot; we do not access your Microsoft 365 mailbox, calendar, or files. [privacy.microsoft.com](https://privacy.microsoft.com)
- **Apple Inc.** (multi-region), iOS app distribution via TestFlight and the Apple App Store, and associated crash and diagnostic reports that you voluntarily share with developers through Apple's system-level settings. [apple.com/legal/privacy](https://apple.com/legal/privacy)

### 5.1 International data transfers

Personal information processed through Best Practices Chatbot is transferred to and stored in the United States, because Supabase, OpenRouter, and (often) Microsoft host their services there. Document ingestion (OCR) may also involve transfer to Mistral AI infrastructure in France. By using Best Practices Chatbot, you acknowledge that your personal information may be subject to the laws of those jurisdictions, including lawful access requests by U.S. government authorities.

We transfer this information in reliance on PIPEDA's accountability-based cross-border transfer framework: we remain accountable for the personal information we transfer to a service provider, and we put in place contractual commitments with each sub-processor that require a comparable level of protection to that required under Canadian law. For any transfer of personal information of individuals located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (or the UK Addendum, as applicable) as the transfer mechanism.

## 6. Data sharing

Personal information collected through Best Practices Chatbot is shared with:

- Onni administrators with administrator access in the App, for the purpose of user approval, support, and managing the corpus;
- the sub-processors listed in Section 5, strictly to perform the technical functions described.

We do not sell personal information. We do not disclose personal information to any other third party except where required by law, court order, or other valid legal process, or where disclosure is necessary to enforce our Terms of Service, protect our rights, or protect the safety of our users.

## 7. Cookies and local storage

Best Practices Chatbot is distributed as a native iOS application and does not use web cookies or browser-based tracking technologies.

**iOS app.** Authentication tokens are stored in the device's secure keychain. The App may cache reference data on the device (for example, the list of documents available and recently viewed source figures) so it can present them quickly without re-downloading. This cached data does not leave the device until you submit a query or open a document, at which point only the request itself is sent to the backend.

## 8. Data retention

We retain account information (email, display name, role, status) for as long as your account is active. Usage events (queries, document views, citations) are retained for as long as needed to operate and improve the App; we periodically aggregate or de-identify older events for analytics purposes.

When an account is deactivated, the identity record (email, name, role) is preserved for a limited period so that the audit trail of historical activity remains attributable, and is thereafter deleted or de-identified. Source documents in the corpus are the property of the Onni Group and are retained according to Onni's internal records-management policies, independently of any user's account.

Server logs and operational telemetry are retained for a shorter period, typically no more than 90 days, unless a specific log is preserved as part of an active security investigation.

## 9. Security

We protect Best Practices Chatbot with a defence-in-depth approach: data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS / TLS) and at rest; access to records is governed by server-side row-level security tied to each user's role and approval status; authentication is performed by Microsoft Entra ID (single sign-on) or, where enabled, by our authentication provider with passwords stored as bcrypt hashes; inputs are validated and rate-limited; and administrator access is provisioned and reviewed manually.

No system is perfectly secure. In the event of a personal information breach that meets the notification threshold under PIPEDA, BC PIPA, or other applicable law, we will notify affected users and the relevant regulator (the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada and the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia) as soon as feasible, and in any event within 72 hours of becoming aware of a breach that creates a real risk of significant harm.

## 10. Your rights

Depending on the jurisdiction in which you live and work, you may have the right to:

- request access to the personal information we hold about you;
- request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information;
- request deletion of personal information that is no longer required for the purpose for which it was collected (subject to the retention obligations described in Section 8);
- withdraw consent for future processing;
- lodge a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia, or your local data protection authority.

**Account deletion.** Because accounts are administered centrally by your organization's administrators, you cannot delete your own account directly from within the App. However, you can request account deletion at any time by:

Emailing [legal@onni.com](mailto:legal@onni.com) with the subject "Account deletion request".

We will acknowledge your request within 5 business days and complete deletion of your account and associated personal information within 30 days of receipt, subject to the retention obligations described in Section 8 (records required for tax, legal, or regulatory recordkeeping may be retained in de-identified form for the periods listed there). To exercise any other rights listed above, contact our Privacy Officer at the address in Section 13, and we will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law (within 30 days under PIPEDA, unless an extension is permitted).
