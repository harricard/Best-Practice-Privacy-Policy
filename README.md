# Privacy Policy — Onni Best Practices

**Effective date:** May 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Onni Contracting Ltd.** ("Onni," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and discloses personal information in connection with the Onni Best Practices mobile application (the "App").

---

## 1. Scope

Onni Best Practices is an internal knowledge tool that lets authorized Onni personnel search and reference Onni's construction best-practice documentation. The App is provided for business use by individuals whose accounts have been provisioned and approved by an Onni administrator. This Policy applies only to the App. It does not apply to the public onni.com website or to any other Onni product, each of which is governed by its own privacy notice.

## 2. Information we collect

### 2.1 Account information

When an account is created for you, or when you sign in, we collect and store:

- your email address;
- your display name;
- authentication identifiers — for email/password sign-in, a securely hashed credential; for Microsoft sign-in, the identifiers returned by your organization's Microsoft Entra ID (Azure AD) directory;
- your account role and status (for example, standard user or administrator, and whether your account is pending, approved, or blocked).

### 2.2 Usage information

To operate the App and understand how it is used, we record application events such as:

- the questions or search queries you submit to the assistant;
- documents and figures you open or view;
- which source documents were returned for a query, and timestamps for these events.

### 2.3 Device and technical information

When you use the App we automatically receive technical information necessary to deliver the service, including network address, app version, and device/operating-system type. We do not use third-party advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs, and the App does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

> The App is not directed to children and is intended only for authorized adult Onni personnel. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of majority in their jurisdiction.

## 3. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

- authenticate you and maintain the security of your account;
- provide the App's core functionality, including retrieving documents and generating answers to your queries;
- administer access — including approving, suspending, or removing accounts;
- understand usage, diagnose problems, and improve the App's reliability and quality;
- comply with applicable law and enforce our [Terms of Service](https://github.com/harricard/Best-Practice-Terms-of-Service).

We do not use your personal information for advertising, and we do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party.

## 4. How information is shared

We disclose personal information only as follows:

- **Within Onni** — with Onni Contracting Ltd. and its affiliated entities, and with Onni administrators, on a need-to-know basis for the purposes described in this Policy;
- **Service providers** — with the vendors listed in Section 5, who process information on our behalf and under contract;
- **Legal and safety** — where required to comply with applicable law, regulation, legal process, or a governmental request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Onni, our personnel, or others;
- **Business transfers** — in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to this Policy.

## 5. Service providers

The App relies on the following third-party processors. Each handles only the data needed to perform its function:

| Provider                          | Function                                          | Data involved                                                          |
| --------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Supabase                          | Authentication, database, and file storage hosting | Account information, usage events, stored documents                    |
| OpenRouter                        | AI model inference for generating answers         | The text of your query and the document passages retrieved for it      |
| Microsoft (Entra ID / Azure AD)   | Optional single sign-on                           | Directory identifiers and basic profile used to authenticate you       |

Information processed by these providers may be stored or processed outside your province or country, including in the United States, and may be subject to the laws of those jurisdictions. We take reasonable steps to ensure providers protect personal information consistent with this Policy.

## 6. Data retention

We retain account information for as long as your account is active. We retain usage information for as long as needed to operate and improve the App. When an account is deleted, associated account and usage records are removed or de-identified within a commercially reasonable period, except where retention is required by law.

## 7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, and you may withdraw consent to our processing. Because Onni Best Practices is an internal business tool, some requests may be coordinated through your Onni administrator. To make a request, contact us using the details in Section 11. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

## 8. Account and data deletion

You may request deletion of your Onni Best Practices account and the personal information associated with it at any time by emailing [privacy@onni.com](mailto:privacy@onni.com) from your Onni Best Practices email address, or by asking your Onni administrator. Upon verification, we will deactivate the account and delete or de-identify the related account and usage records, except for information we are required to retain by law.

## 9. Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information, including encrypted transport, access controls, and row-level security on stored data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your information and to address incidents promptly.

## 10. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Effective date" above and, where appropriate, provide additional notice within the App. Your continued use of the App after an update takes effect constitutes acceptance of the revised Policy.
