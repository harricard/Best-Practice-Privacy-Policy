# Privacy Policy — Best Practices Chatbot

**Effective date:** May 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how **Onni Contracting Ltd.** ("Onni," "we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and discloses personal information in connection with the Best Practices Chatbot mobile application (the "App").

---

## 1. Scope

Best Practices Chatbot is an internal knowledge tool that lets authorized Onni personnel search and reference Onni's construction best-practice documentation. The App is provided for business use by individuals whose accounts have been provisioned and approved by an Onni administrator. This Policy applies only to the App. It does not apply to the public onni.com website or to any other Onni product, each of which is governed by its own privacy notice.

Best Practices Chatbot is not a consumer service. Accounts are provisioned by an administrator; there is no self-signup.

## 2. Information we collect

All data collected by Best Practices Chatbot is linked to an authenticated user account and is collected solely to operate the service. We do not use any of this data for advertising, cross-app tracking, or profiling. The App contains no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

### 2.1 Account information

When an account is created for you, or when you sign in, we collect and store:

- your email address;
- your display name;
- authentication identifiers — for email/password sign-in, a securely hashed credential; for Microsoft sign-in, the identifiers returned by your organization's Microsoft Entra ID (Azure AD) directory;
- your account role and status (for example, standard user or administrator, and whether your account is pending, approved, or blocked).

### 2.2 Usage information

To operate the App and understand how it is used, we record application events such as:

- the questions or search queries you submit to the assistant;
- documents and figures you open or view;
- which source documents were returned for a query, and timestamps for these events.

### 2.3 Device and technical information

When you use the App we automatically receive technical information necessary to deliver the service, including network address, app version, and device/operating-system type. We do not use third-party advertising or cross-app tracking SDKs, and the App does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies.

> The App is not directed to children and is intended only for authorized adult Onni personnel. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of majority in their jurisdiction.

### 2.4 Information we do not collect

We do not collect device identifiers for advertising (IDFA / Google AID), location data, contacts, health data, financial data, browsing history, or sensitive personal information. We do not sell personal information, and we do not share personal information with third parties for their independent marketing purposes.

## 3. How we use information

We use the information described above to:

- authenticate you and maintain the security of your account;
- provide the App's core functionality, including retrieving documents and generating answers to your queries;
- administer access — including approving, suspending, or removing accounts;
- understand usage, diagnose problems, and improve the App's reliability and quality;
- comply with applicable law and enforce our [Terms of Service](https://harricard.github.io/Best-Practice-Terms-of-Service/).

We do not use your personal information for advertising, and we do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party.

## 4. Legal bases (PIPEDA, BC PIPA, GDPR)

Best Practices Chatbot is operated from Canada and processes personal information under the federal Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and the British Columbia Personal Information Protection Act (BC PIPA). Because the App is a business tool used in the context of an employment or contractor relationship, the personal information we handle is largely work-product information and business contact information, for which a more limited consent regime applies.

Where the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or the United Kingdom GDPR applies to a user, our legal bases for processing are: (a) performance of a contract with the user's employer, (b) our legitimate interest in operating an internal knowledge tool, and (c) compliance with legal obligations. Best Practices Chatbot is not directed to or intended for use by children, and we do not knowingly process personal information of anyone under the age of majority.

## 5. How information is shared

We disclose personal information only as follows:

- **Within Onni** — with Onni Contracting Ltd. and its affiliated entities, and with Onni administrators, on a need-to-know basis for the purposes described in this Policy;
- **Service providers** — with the vendors listed in Section 6, who process information on our behalf and under contract;
- **Legal and safety** — where required to comply with applicable law, regulation, legal process, or a governmental request, or to protect the rights, property, or safety of Onni, our personnel, or others;
- **Business transfers** — in connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, subject to this Policy.

### 6.1 International data transfers

Personal information processed through Best Practices Chatbot is transferred to and stored in the United States, because Supabase, OpenRouter, and (often) Microsoft host their services there. Mistral AI is based in France (EU). By using the App, you acknowledge that your personal information may be subject to the laws of those jurisdictions, including lawful access requests by U.S. government authorities.

We transfer this information in reliance on PIPEDA's accountability-based cross-border transfer framework: we remain accountable for the personal information we transfer to a service provider, and we put in place contractual commitments with each sub-processor that require a comparable level of protection to that required under Canadian law. For any transfer of personal information of individuals located in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses (or the UK Addendum, as applicable) as the transfer mechanism.

## 7. Data retention

We retain account information for as long as your account is active. We retain usage information for as long as needed to operate and improve the App. When an account is deleted, associated account and usage records are removed or de-identified within a commercially reasonable period, except where retention is required by law.

Server logs and operational telemetry are retained for a shorter period, typically no more than 90 days, unless a specific log is preserved as part of an active security investigation.

## 8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

- request access to the personal information we hold about you;
- request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal information;
- request deletion of personal information that is no longer required for the purpose for which it was collected (subject to the retention obligations described in Section 7);
- withdraw consent for future processing;
- lodge a complaint with the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia, or your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Privacy Officer at the address in Section 13. We will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law (within 30 days under PIPEDA, unless an extension is permitted).

## 9. Account and data deletion

Because accounts are administered centrally by your organization's administrators, you cannot delete your own account directly from within the App. You may request deletion of your Best Practices Chatbot account and the personal information associated with it at any time by emailing [legal@onni.com](mailto:legal@onni.com) with the subject "Account deletion request" from your Best Practices Chatbot email address, or by asking your Onni administrator.

We will acknowledge your request within 5 business days and complete deletion of your account and associated personal information within 30 days of receipt, subject to the retention obligations described in Section 7.

## 10. Security

We protect Best Practices Chatbot with a defence-in-depth approach: data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest, access to records is governed by server-side permission rules (row-level security) tied to each user's role and approval status, authentication is performed by our authentication provider and (optionally) Microsoft Entra ID, and administrator access is provisioned and reviewed manually.

No system is perfectly secure. In the event of a personal information breach that meets the notification threshold under PIPEDA, BC PIPA, or other applicable law, we will notify affected users and the relevant regulator (the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada and the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner for British Columbia) as soon as feasible, and in any event within 72 hours of becoming aware of a breach that creates a real risk of significant harm.
